# ORBIT demo video: "Rewind" (30 s)

Format limit: video up to 30 seconds, English subtitles required, hosted on a public link (2025 submission guide; re-check the 2026 guide on Nov 13).

The day counter is the thread of the video: it runs forward, rewinds, then runs forward again.

| Time | On screen | Voice-over (+ subtitles) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Counter: Day 1 ✓, Day 2 ✓, then it speeds up (50, 120, 300…). Digital twin stays green. Soft ticking. | "Day 1. Day 2. Everything's fine." |
| 3–6 s | Counter stops dead on **DAY 412**. Suit check before EVA, red alarm. | "Day 412. Nova skips a step in the suit check." |
| 6–8 s | Freeze frame, 1 s of silence, then the counter rewinds: 411… 410… 409. | "How did it come to this?" |
| 8–12 s | **Day 409**: mission timeline, X5.8 flare spike, label *NASA DONKI · May 2024*. | "A real solar storm hits. The crew rushes into the shelter." |
| 12–16 s | **Days 410–411**: sleep drops, check-in says *"I feel fine"*, PVT reaction times plunge, self-report gap lights up. | "Sleep collapses. Nova says: I'm fine. The reaction times disagree." |
| 16–20 s | Twin turns orange, alert with SHAP bars, assistant citing NASA-STD-3001 with an **offline** badge. | "ORBIT saw it coming, and explained why." |
| 20–25 s | What-if: sliders move. **0 lapses**, **radiation limit day 532 → 545**. | "What if Nova had listened? More sleep, more shelter: zero lapses." |
| 25–28 s | Counter runs forward again to **DAY 412**. Same suit check, all green ✓. | "Day 412. Same suit check. Different ending." |
| 28–30 s | ORBIT logo, team names, NASA Space Apps 2026. | "ORBIT. Because on Mars, you can't call a doctor." |

About 75 words.

## Production notes

- One big element per shot (a number or a word), otherwise it is unreadable in 4 seconds.
- Ticking speeds up with the counter, then stops right before the day 412 alarm: half a second of silence.
- Shown on screen rather than spoken, to save time: "NASA DONKI", "offline", "+13 days".
- The suit-check shots (0–6 s, 25–28 s) are not app screens: a simple title card or checklist turning red, then green.
- If timing is too tight, the last line can be on-screen text only.
- Voice recorded by a team member. Any music must be royalty-free and cited in SOURCES.md.
- Must show: team name, both members, the chosen challenge, and how NASA open data (DONKI) was used.
